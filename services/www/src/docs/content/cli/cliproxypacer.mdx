A chat on the [CLIProxyPacer provider](/providers#cliproxypacer) gets four extras in the TUI: a cache item in the
toolbar, a **Usage** section in the sidebar, a **Requests** tab in the footer, and three switches. Every TUI also
shows [cache notices](#cache-notices), whatever chat it shows, and **Settings** gets a [CLIProxyPacer](#settings)
section. Without CLIProxyPacer set up, none of these shows.

- The **Requests** tab shows for a chat that used CLIProxyPacer: its model is one of CLIProxyPacer's, or one of its
  steps went through CLIProxyPacer.
- The cache item and the switches show only while the chat's model is one of CLIProxyPacer's: the model selected for
  it, else the one its last reply came from. The **Usage** section shows while the model picked in the prompt is one of
  CLIProxyPacer's. A chat moved to another provider keeps its **Requests** tab.

```text
warm 23:41
```

The keys are listed on [Keybinds](/cli/keybinds#cliproxypacer).

## Toolbar

The toolbar under the prompt shows one cache item after the context and cost. It counts down to when the chat's prompt
cache goes cold if nothing more is sent, including the pings CLIProxyPacer still plans.

| It shows                                         | When                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `warm 23:41`                                     | the cache is warm and CLIProxyPacer pings it                                                                                                                                      |
| `warming off · warm 3:12`                        | warming is off for the chat; the cache is still warm                                                                                                                              |
| `cache cold`                                     | the cache went cold, shown as a warning                                                                                                                                           |
| `cache cold · compacts first`                    | the cache went cold, [compact cold chats first](/providers#compaction-asks) and automatic compaction are on for the chat, and the chat is large enough for it, shown as a warning |
| `keep-alive stopped · warm 2:10`                 | CLIProxyPacer's pings stopped on a failure, shown as a warning                                                                                                                    |
| `compacts at 14:32`                              | compact before cold is on, and CLIProxyPacer has set the time it asks for it                                                                                                      |
| `account used up · next step moves to <account>` | the chat's account can't serve its next step, shown as a warning                                                                                                                  |
| `warm`                                           | the cache is warm and CLIProxyPacer gives no time                                                                                                                                 |
| nothing                                          | the proxy has no pacer, CLIProxyPacer knows nothing of the chat yet, or the chat's model is another provider's                                                                    |

- While CLIProxyPacer is unreachable the item keeps its last text, greyed, with the time it is from:
  `warm 12:05 · as of 14:02`. It stops counting until CLIProxyPacer answers again.
- In a narrow window the shortcuts go first, then the context and cost; the cache item goes last.
- Click it to open the footer at the **Requests** tab.

## Sidebar

The **Usage** section, above the sidebar's other sections, shows how much is left on the accounts that serve the chat's
model, from the provider CLIProxyPacer serves it from. The sidebar's **Context** section is gone; the toolbar shows the
same.

```text
Usage
Claude Max 20x   5h                7d
● ████░░░  62%   1h ██████░  80%  ~3d
  ███████ 100%   5h ███████ 100%   7d
Claude Pro       5h                7d
› █░░░░░░  12%  46m ██░░░░░  31%   2d
● in use  › next use
```

- Accounts are grouped by provider and plan (`Claude Max 20x`, `Claude Pro`), one row per account, without its name.
  On the group's line, each column names its window: its length, after its scope when it covers only some models
  (`opus 7d`).
- Only the windows that limit the chat's model show: an Antigravity account shows its Claude and GPT windows for a
  Claude model, its Gemini windows for a Gemini one.
- Each window is a bar of what is left, the percent left (rounded down) and the time to its reset: `46m`, `1h`, `2d`
  (hours and days rounded down); `~` marks a reset CLIProxyPacer estimates. A window nothing has used since its reset
  shows its length (`5h`), `now` says the reset has passed with no reading since. With three windows the percent goes
  to make room (the bar still shows it), except where no reset is known, where it takes the time's place.
- A bar is green above 20% left, yellow at 20% or less and red at 10% or less; grey while the reading isn't current
  (unused since its reset, past its reset, or older than an hour, when CLIProxyPacer stops counting on it).
- `●` marks the account serving the chat: green while its step runs (from its first step on), blue for the one that
  served it last. `›` marks where the chat goes once its cache is cold (blue), or where CLIProxyPacer must move it (a
  warning). A line under the groups names the marks shown.
- It follows the model picked in the prompt, before you send. Until CLIProxyPacer has served the chat that model (a
  new chat, or one just switched to another model), it shows every provider that serves the model, API keys included.
  When the chat's next account is another provider's, that provider shows too.
- When only some of a provider's accounts serve the model (Command Code's API key serves Claude models, its logins
  don't), only the accounts CLIProxyPacer names for the chat show: its own and where it moves. CLIProxyPacer tells how
  many accounts serve a model, not which.
- While CLIProxyPacer is unreachable, the section is greyed and ends with `as of 14:02`. On a proxy without the pacer it
  says `no pacer`.
- It polls nothing: the meters come with CLIProxyPacer's change stream, as they are read. A chat's view is read once
  more on its first step, to mark the account in use.

## Requests tab

The **Requests** tab lists the chat's requests at CLIProxyPacer, with its subagents', newest at the top. Press **↓**
from the prompt to open the footer, then **←/→** to reach the tab after **Terminals**, or click the toolbar's cache
item. Opening a [cache notice](#cache-notices) opens it too.

```text
time      kind        model               account       read/write    verdict                              cost
14:02:13  step        claude-sonnet-5-5   claude-max-2  48.2K/1.0K    hit                               $0.0312
14:01:40  explore     claude-haiku-5-5    claude-max-2  0/12.5K       miss · prefix-changed · tools[0]  $0.0451
14:00:02  title       claude-haiku-5-5    claude-max-2  0/0                                             $0.0004
```

- **kind** is `step`, the agent of a subagent's step, `ping`, `title`, `compaction` (a summary) or `search`, a call of
  one of CLIProxyPacer's [web tools](#web-tools): its model is the tool, its account the service's key.
- **read/write** are the tokens read from and written to the prompt cache.
- **verdict** is CLIProxyPacer's cache verdict. A miss shows its cause and the part of the prompt that changed, as a
  warning; a failed or refused request shows as an error.
- The columns are as wide as their values need, so a value is cut only when the line has no room left. They keep their
  widths while the tab stays open; a longer value that arrives meanwhile is cut until you open the tab again or resize
  the window.
- In a narrow window the model goes first, then the account, then read/write.
- With the newest row selected, a new request takes the selection, so the newest stays under the cursor. With any other
  row selected, the selection stays on its request as new rows arrive at the top.
- More rows are read as you near the end of the list.

| Key                             | Does                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **↑/↓**, **Page Up/Down**       | Move between requests; **↑** on the newest closes the footer.                                                                                                                 |
| **Ctrl+A**                      | Shows only cache misses, or all requests again. The TUI remembers it until it exits, as it does the Subagents filter.                                                         |
| **Enter**                       | Moves the chat to where the request ended: the end of that step's reply. A subagent's request opens the subagent's chat there; a ping or a title goes to the reply before it. |
| **Ctrl+Enter** or **Alt+Enter** | Shows what changed in the request's prompt, in place of the list. The same key or **Esc** goes back to the same row.                                                          |

What changed shows CLIProxyPacer's verdict and its cause, how many tokens were written to the cache again and what the
miss cost, and the request it was compared with. Below that come the parts of the prompt that changed, are new, or only
had their cache marker moved. It scrolls with **↑/↓**, and the footer grows to at most half the window. When
CLIProxyPacer no longer keeps the prompt, it says `prompt no longer kept`. A `search` row has none: a web tool call
sends no prompt, so what changed says so and reads nothing, and **Enter** on it goes to the chat's first step after the
call.

```text
miss · prefix-changed · tools[0] changed
12.5K tokens written again · the miss cost $0.0389
compared with 1793911200-claude-max-2-7f3a of 14:00:02
changed  tool       tools[0]  1.2K chars  read: Read a file from the local filesystem
14 unchanged
```

**Inside tmux use Alt+Enter.** tmux passes a modified Enter on only with its `extended-keys` option; without it,
Ctrl+Enter can arrive as a plain Enter, which moves the chat to the request instead. Alt+Enter gets through. In macOS
Terminal it needs **Use Option as Meta key** in the profile's keyboard settings.

The tab says why it is empty: `No requests yet`, `No cache misses yet`, `CLIProxyPacer unreachable · as of 14:02` above the last
rows, greyed, or `This proxy has no pacer, so it keeps no request records`. It is hidden when CLIProxyPacer isn't set
up or the chat never used it.

## Cache notices

When a step or a summary of one of your chats misses CLIProxyPacer's prompt cache, or reuses only part of it, for a
reason you wouldn't expect, a card shows in the top-right corner of every open TUI, whatever chat it shows. It says what
was lost, in which chat, and why.

```text
Cache miss · 152K tokens not reused        3 min ago ×
Fix the login flow · build · claude-sonnet-5-5
prefix-changed · tools[0]
```

- The first line says `Cache miss` with a red edge or `Partial cache reuse` with a yellow one, the tokens not reused
  when CLIProxyPacer counts them, the card's age (`just now`, `3 min ago`) and `×`.
- The second names the chat, its agent and its model; a subagent's card names the subagent's chat.
- The third is the cause, as the **Requests** tab says it, or `unknown cause` when CLIProxyPacer gives none.
- At most 4 cards show, fewer in a short terminal, then `+2 more cache notices`. While a dialog is open they wait
  behind it.

| To                 | Do                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open a card        | Click it, or press `<leader>o` for the newest. The TUI goes to the chat that sent the request, opens its **Requests** tab on that request and shows what changed in it. |
| Dismiss a card     | Click its `×`. An opened card is dismissed too.                                                                                                                         |
| Dismiss every card | **Dismiss cache notices** in the command palette (**Ctrl+P**). It has no key until you bind `pacer.notices.dismiss`.                                                    |

What raises a card, and its limits:

- Only steps and summaries raise one, a subagent's included: pings, titles and web tool calls don't.
- The losses you can expect raise none: the cache went cold (CLIProxyPacer's cause `expired`; the toolbar says
  `cache cold` then), or a summary replaced the prompt (`compaction`), as in the first step after a compaction. The
  **Requests** tab still lists them, with their cause, such as `miss · expired`. Every other cause raises a card, and
  so does a miss or partial reuse without one.
- Only this service's chats: another client's requests on the same key raise none.
- The background service keeps the cards in memory, the newest 50 of the last 2 hours. A restart of the service starts
  with none, and requests CLIProxyPacer received before it started raise none.
- The cards stay while CLIProxyPacer is unreachable. Requests read after it answers again still raise their cards, if
  they are within the card's lifetime.
- There is no sound and no system notification.

Each TUI shows the cards its own settings let through. They are rows of [Settings](#settings) and keys of `cli.json`
([`cliproxypacer.notices`](/cli/config#cliproxypacer)):

| Row                            | Choices                                | Default                  |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Cache notices                  | on, off                                | on                       |
| Cache notices from             | all chats, this chat and its subagents | all chats                |
| Cache notice lifetime          | 15, 30, 60, 120 minutes                | 15 minutes               |
| Cache notice minimum           | any loss, 1K, 10K, 50K, 100K tokens    | any loss                 |
| Cache notices for              | misses and partial reuse, misses only  | misses and partial reuse |
| Opened cache notice disappears | in every TUI, only in this TUI         | in every TUI             |

- With **Cache notices** off, the TUI reads and shows none.
- **This chat and its subagents** shows the cards of the chat you look at, the chat it belongs to and their subagents.
  The home screen then shows none.
- A card leaves at its lifetime: with 15 minutes, its last age is `14 min ago`.
- A card without a token count shows only with **any loss**.
- **Only in this TUI** keeps the dismissal in the TUI's own storage for a day. Every TUI on the same computer shares
  that storage; a TUI elsewhere keeps showing the card.

## Settings

Press **Ctrl+P**, then select **Open settings**. While CLIProxyPacer answers, **Settings** has a **CLIProxyPacer**
section with ten rows. Change a row with **Enter** or **←/→**.

```text
CLIProxyPacer
Cache notices                   on
Cache notices from              all chats
Cache notice lifetime           15 minutes
Cache notice minimum            any loss
Cache notices for               misses and partial reuse
Opened cache notice disappears  in every TUI
Compact cold chats first        off
Compact before cold             off
Summaries written by            CLIProxyPacer
Keep my messages in summaries   off
```

- The first six are this TUI's own, for its [cache notices](#cache-notices), saved in `cli.json`.
- The last four are CLIProxyPacer's settings for every chat on your client key, kept by CLIProxyPacer: see
  [Compaction asks](/providers#compaction-asks) and [Summaries](/providers#summaries).

| Row                           | Choices                  | Default at CLIProxyPacer                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Compact cold chats first      | on, off                  | off, unless your CLIProxyPacer's server turns it on |
| Compact before cold           | on, off                  | off, unless your CLIProxyPacer's server turns it on |
| Summaries written by          | CLIProxyPacer, openforge | CLIProxyPacer                                       |
| Keep my messages in summaries | on, off                  | off                                                 |

- The section shows only while CLIProxyPacer answers. It hides while CLIProxyPacer isn't set up, is unreachable, or is a
  proxy without the pacer; change the six notice rows in `cli.json` then.
- CLIProxyPacer's four rows are read from it when Settings opens, and show what applies to your chats: your key's own
  value, else the server's, else CLIProxyPacer's default. The two summaries rows show only when CLIProxyPacer lists
  those settings, which an older CLIProxyPacer doesn't.
- Switching one of them to the value CLIProxyPacer applies without yours removes yours, so that a value set later for
  the whole server reaches your chats.
- A chat's own switch, `<leader>f` or `<leader>j`, wins over its row.
- A change CLIProxyPacer refuses or can't take shows its error as a toast.

## Switches

Three switches change how CLIProxyPacer treats the chat. The leader is **Ctrl+X** unless you changed it, so
`<leader>k` is **Ctrl+X**, then **K**. Each is also in the command palette (**Ctrl+P**), under CLIProxyPacer.

| Key         | Palette                                | Switches                                                                                        |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `<leader>k` | Cache warming for this chat            | CLIProxyPacer's pings for the chat, which keep its prompt cache warm while you pause.           |
| `<leader>j` | Compact before cold for this chat      | Whether CLIProxyPacer asks to compact the idle chat before its cache goes cold.                 |
| `<leader>f` | Compact cold chats first for this chat | Whether CLIProxyPacer asks to compact the chat, once its cache went cold, before its next step. |

```text
Compact cold chats first on for this chat
```

- A toast says the new state, and the toolbar shows it at once.
- A chat's switch wins over the row for all chats in [Settings](#settings).
- A switch changed elsewhere, such as on CLIProxyPacer's own page, shows the next time the TUI shows the chat.
- Before the chat's first reply, CLIProxyPacer knows nothing of it yet: switch it after that reply.
- On a proxy without the pacer, or for a chat whose model is another provider's, neither the keys nor the palette
  entries exist.

What the two compaction asks do, and their limits, is on [Compaction asks](/providers#compaction-asks).

**Warm while waiting** needs no switch. While a chat waits on a command in the foreground or on a subagent, openforge
asks CLIProxyPacer to keep pinging it for up to 4 hours, and stops when the wait ends. Commands in the background don't
count.

## Summaries

A compaction of a chat on CLIProxyPacer names who wrote its summary on its divider in the chat:

```text
Compaction · by CLIProxyPacer on gemini-3.8-flash-high · cold · 12 of your messages kept · 20,182 in · 214 out
```

- `on <model>` is the model that wrote it.
- `cold` says CLIProxyPacer wrote it on its cheaper model because the chat was cold.
- `12 of your messages kept` shows with **Keep my messages in summaries** on: your earlier messages CLIProxyPacer kept
  word for word after the summary.
- Then come the tokens of the summary request. Each part shows only when it is known.
- In a narrow window the tokens go first, then the messages kept, the model, and `cold`.

When openforge wrote the summary, the divider says so, and why when CLIProxyPacer was meant to:

```text
Compaction · by openforge · CLIProxyPacer couldn't: it answered 501 · 48,210 in · 1,904 out
```

Who writes a summary, and when openforge does, is on [Summaries](/providers#summaries).

## Web tools

When CLIProxyPacer has web tools, every chat gets them, also a chat on another provider's model: `web_search`,
`web_research`, `web_extract` and `web_crawl` (see [Web tools](/providers#web-tools)). They show in the chat as tool
calls.

- A call asks first, as the built-in web search and `webfetch` do (see [Approvals](/permissions#approvals)).
- A call CLIProxyPacer can't serve shows as a failed tool call, with CLIProxyPacer's reason, which the model reads too.
- A research or a crawl that outlasts one call shows the job's ID; the model's next call of the tool, with that ID, gets
  the result.
